## Watch — the real windows

- **Public Benefit Innovation Fund** (Center for Civic Futures) — $20M fund, $8.5M
  already committed. Two tracks: **Early Concept / R&D, up to $500K, 6–12 months**;

  determination, renewal, delivery; enrollment gaps, administrative churn, error

  work around it?" No signed government partner needed at application. Contact
