**Brief Solution Description:**
CNA Shield is a voice-first mobile AI application that gives Certified Nursing Assistants hands-free tools to document care delivery, generate structured shift handoffs, and report staffing violations — without stopping their work to type. By adapting proven clinical AI documentation technology for the direct care workforce, CNA Shield reduces documentation burden, closes dangerous shift communication gaps, and creates the timestamped objective record that currently does not exist to protect workers when systems fail them. Analysis of 1,324 posts from CNA communities confirms documentation burden, unsafe staffing ratios, and shift handoff failures as the dominant workforce pain points.
