Both tracks, Dementia Coach (Track 1) and CNA Shield (Track 2), lost. The challenge is
closed for us: there is no Phase 2 and no resubmission.

## The numbers

- **18 winners of ~650 = 2.8%.** 9 per track at $100,000 each, plus 6 meritorious awards of
  $50,000 (4 in Track 1, 2 in Track 2). Source: acl.gov/caregiver-ai-challenge/winners,
  read 2026-11-19.
- **Our written estimate was ~15% primary, ~25–30% meritorious** (memory `nia-sbir-pursuit`,
  2026-07-30). That was more than 5× too generous. The estimate was a soft guess, and the real base rate was 2.8%.

## Winners in our two lanes

**CNA Shield's lane (voice shift notes → structured record).** 4 of the 9 Track 2 winners do this:

| Winner | What sets it apart, per ACL's own summary |
|---|---|
| Care Rounds* (Juno Code Studio) | checks off tasks on the client's **care plan**; aide approves every entry; client consent before recording |
| CareScribe (Lynxsen) | **Medicaid** home care; English + **Spanish**; checks against the care plan; agencies catch errors **before billing** |
| Remy CareNotes (Remy Camera) | built **with a home care agency owner**; a study of 15,000+ caregiver app reviews; a morning briefing for managers |
| Terry (Terry Health) | DSPs, any language → **Medicaid-aligned** English; **pilot of ~100 staff, 35 min → ~3 min per note** |

**Dementia Coach's lane (coaching a caregiver through a hard moment).**

| Winner | What sets it apart |
|---|---|
| iana Care Companion* (ianacare) | trained on interactions from **50,000+ families**; a human Care Navigator closes the loop |
| Homeport* (Corvandi Labs) | real-time coaching for DSPs during escalation; **agency's own procedures**; unsafe → routed to a person |
| Adaptive Training (Ojje) | **six Colorado partners**; includes dementia scenarios |

`*` = meritorious award.

## What this says, and what it doesn't

- **The shape was right.** ACL funded four separate teams to build CNA Shield's core idea.
  That confirms the premise. It says nothing about adoption (BUSINESS.md rule 2).
- **The winners had what we didn't**: an operating partner or a pilot with numbers, a link to
  the care plan or to Medicaid billing, and multilingual input. A TRL log was not the
  differentiator. Aklia won Track 1 with 87.5% accuracy over 40 cycles, and CNA Shield
  reported 98%.
- **Kris Foss (ICI/UMN) letter of support**: it was a real asset and still exists. It was
  written for the ACL prize, so any new use needs a new letter.

## Not affected

- Both prototypes stay live (cna-shield.vercel.app, dementia-coach.vercel.app).
- Publicity rules no longer matter. The only claim we ever had was "applied", and there is
  now no award or finalist claim to make.
