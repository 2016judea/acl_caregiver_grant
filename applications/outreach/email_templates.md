# Cold-Outreach Email Templates

Three templates. All deliberately short (<160 words), lead with the prototype + the
specific data finding, and make the ask an easy yes with an easy out. Swap the `[ ]` fields.

Pick the ask per contact: **feedback** (lowest bar, warms them up), **named advisor**
(what moves the team table), or **letter of support** (what fills the appendix).

---

## Template 1 — Advisor ask, dementia/clinical (Dementia Coach)

**Subject:** Dementia caregiver AI tool — would value 20 min of your expertise

turns validation therapy into real-time, personalized communication scripts for family

most-addressable gap in family caregiver support.

understand if your plate is full.

Either way, thank you for your work on [the BOLD Center / dementia caregiving].

Aidan Jude
[phone] · [email]

### Tips
- Send in small batches; personalize the one bracketed clause that names *their* work.
- Follow up once after ~5 business days — most replies come on the nudge, not the first send.
- If they say yes to "feedback," the advisor/letter ask gets much easier on the call.
