**Subject:** Next PBIF open call — and a scope question

I missed the spring call. I'd like to know when the next one opens.

And a scope question while I wait.

I build public-record data infrastructure in Minnesota — county parcels, recorded
sales, assessor values, land use, joined into one queryable model. Seven counties,
1,131,534 parcels, published free.

Medicaid prohibits a provider from holding a financial interest in a beneficiary's
housing. DOJ's May 2026 Individualized Home Supports case charged two defendants
who acquired over 20 separate residences and concealed the ownership interest from
Medicaid. The state didn't have that ownership record. The county did, the whole
time — every deed, recorded, public, sitting in a system nobody joined to the
provider file.

In Hennepin County, only 57 owners hold 20 or more residential parcels. That is a
list a program-integrity team reads in an afternoon, not a needle in a haystack.

So: is program integrity in scope for PBIF — improper payments on the delivery
side — or is the fund squarely on enrollment, churn and processing time?
