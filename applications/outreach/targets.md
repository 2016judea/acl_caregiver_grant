## Notes on the ask
- **Advisor ≠ employee.** You need a *named commitment to advise*, not payroll.
- **Letters are cold asks, not favors.** A working prototype + real data is your hook.
- Lead every email with the prototype link + the specific finding — that's your credibility.
- Mix big names (Gaugler) with reachable mid-level staff (coordinators, junior faculty,
  support-group facilitators) — the latter reply more often.
